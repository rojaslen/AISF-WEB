# Ruxpin Retrofit (YouTube playlist)

##Description
This video series will document experiments in replacing Teddy's 1985 tape deck system with a modern voice-interactive LLM. This is a fully local, non-cloud, device-only system, custom-trained for child safety.

Following hardware replacement, I will attempt to revive and synchronize the animatronic movement to the LLM's speech output. 

Part of the AI Stability Framework (AISF) project, link in the channel description.
